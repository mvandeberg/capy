  (Doxygen comments in `include/boost/capy/**` headers, source for the MrDocs reference),
  or `both`.

  `3`=Completeness/Pedagogy, `4`=Wording, `5`=Corosio (repeat of Phases 0–4 against the

- `owner` — `unassigned` for Phase 1–4 rows (picked up task-by-task per the plan);
  `Phase 5` for Corosio-deferred rows (require the Corosio repo checked out).
